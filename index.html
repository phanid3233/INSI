About the group

One group that surveys, clears and builds.

INSI Group: Development, Construction, UXO & Engineering Co., Ltd is headquartered in Vientiane Capital, Lao PDR. It brings three sole subsidiaries together so a project can move from the first survey to handover with a single accountable partner.

Our clients include Électricité du Laos, ministries of the Lao government and international development partners such as KfW, AFD and ADB.

Questions

What clients ask before they tender.

What does the 3 in 1 model mean?

One group covers survey and engineering design, UXO clearance and civil construction. You deal with one accountable partner from feasibility to handover, so there is no gap between the designer, the clearance team and the builder.

Can I hire only one subsidiary?

Yes. Each subsidiary is a sole company with its own contracts and track record. Select one, two or all three divisions in the inquiry form.

Which standards govern your UXO work?

Operations follow the Lao National Regulatory Authority requirements and the International Mine Action Standards (IMAS).

Where have you worked?

Our register covers Vientiane Capital and Province, Xaysomboun, Xiengkhouang, Savannakhet, Saravan, Champasak, Sekong, Luang Prabang, Khammouane and Xayaboury. Browse the full list in the portfolio.

Who are your clients?

Électricité du Laos, ministries and provincial departments, projects funded by KfW, AFD and ADB, and private developers and energy investors.
